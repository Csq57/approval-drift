## What you are getting

Approval drift is provided **as is**, without warranty of any kind. It records the values you
approve and flags when those values change. It is a bookkeeping tool for a decision that a human
made — it does not approve, authorise, or block anything by itself.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for the values you enter and for acting on what the app reports. A stale
approval is a signal to re-check, not an automatic failure; a matching approval is not a substitute
for your own controls.
